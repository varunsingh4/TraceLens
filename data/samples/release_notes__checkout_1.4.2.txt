Release notes: checkout-service 1.4.2
Shipped: 2026-10-20 13:58 UTC

Changes:
- Lowered PaymentAuthorizationClient timeout from 8000ms to 2500ms to fail faster.
- Removed one retry on /authorize because duplicate charges were reported in staging.
- Added structured logging for trace ids on checkout 5xx responses.

Known risk:
Checkout is now more sensitive to payments-service latency spikes. A p95 above 2.5s
will surface as 500s instead of slow 200s.
